BTC Field App — Privacy Policy

Package: ge.btc.field_app · Effective date: 1 November 2026

Englishქართული

English

BTC Field App ("the app") is a landscape field-survey tool published by the Georgian Cartographers Association ("GCA", "we"). This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

1. Data stored only on your device

  • Survey records (site name, date, coordinates, altitude, aspect, relief, soil and vegetation measurements, notes) are stored in a local database on your device.
  • Photos you take with the camera or pick from your gallery are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Photos are not uploaded to our servers.
  • GPS tracks, settings and added map layers are stored on your device.

2. Data sent to services

DataSent toPurpose
Email address and password (password handled by Firebase, never stored by us) Google Firebase AuthenticationOptional sign-in for cloud sync
Survey records, including their coordinates (signed-in users only) Google Cloud FirestoreBackup and sharing records within an expedition team

App version checkGitHub APINotifying you about updates

Analytics events do not contain your survey content, coordinates or email address. Location is used only while the app is in use; the app does not collect location in the background.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising. The app contains no ads. Records you sync to an expedition are visible to the members of that expedition. When you export or email records (CSV, GeoJSON, KML, GPX, PDF), the data goes only to the recipients or apps you choose.

4. Permissions

  • Location — to place records and tracks on the map.
  • Camera / photos — to attach photos to records.
  • Internet — maps, weather, sign-in, sync.

5. Security and retention

Data in transit to Google Firebase is encrypted (TLS). Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Cloud data is kept while your account exists.

6. Deleting your data and account

  • Delete individual records in the app; deleted records are also removed from the cloud.
  • In the app: Settings → Data Storage → Delete account. After you confirm with your password, your account and all records stored in your cloud space are deleted immediately. Records on your device and records shared into an expedition are kept (ask the expedition lead or email us to remove them).
  • Without the app: to delete your account and all associated cloud data, email kapan.gio777@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "BTC Field App account deletion". We delete the account and its records within 30 days. Anonymous analytics data is retained by Firebase for up to 14 months.

7. Children

The app is intended for researchers and students and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change. Questions: kapan.gio777@gmail.com · Georgian Cartographers Association, Tbilisi, Georgia · source code.

Google's handling of Firebase data: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
